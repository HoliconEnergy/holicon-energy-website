OUR APPROACH

A coordinated approach from specification to handover

We aim to identify interfaces early so trades are not solving avoidable issues at the end of the programme. That includes who provides AC supplies, isolators, cable routes, access and suitable mounting locations.

Information that helps us price accurately

When sending a development enquiry, the most helpful starting point is a current drawing package and a clear picture of the installation programme. We can then discuss a scope appropriate to the plots.

  • Plot list, roof plans and building types
  • Proposed panel quantities or performance requirements
  • Electrical specification and inverter locations
  • Scaffolding, access arrangements and target dates

GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

Can Holicon price an entire housing development?

Yes, we can discuss grouped plot packages, phasing and clearly defined installation responsibilities based on the project information supplied.

Do you work with self-build homeowners?

Yes. Early design conversations can help reserve the right roof and electrical provisions for solar PV, batteries and EV charging.

When should solar PV be considered on a new build?

Ideally during design and specification, before roof and electrical arrangements are finalised. Earlier coordination can reduce rework.

Can you help with documentation and handover?

Installation documentation, commissioning and relevant certification should be agreed as part of the project scope so every plot has a clear handover route.
