ONE COORDINATED APPROACH

One property. One joined-up renewables plan.

We look at how the property will generate, store and use energy before the key building services decisions are locked in. That means thinking about plant locations, roof design, electrical capacity, future occupants and how each system works alongside the others.

Working with established heating and electrical specialists where required, we can coordinate the renewable specification around your construction programme — from a single self-build to a multi-plot development.

What can be included?

  • Solar PV: roof-integrated planning, panel layout and generation
  • Battery storage: storage options, inverter compatibility and future provision
  • EV charging: charger positioning, electrical infrastructure and smart charging considerations
  • Heat pumps: heating-system integration and coordination with the wider building services specification
  • Handover: commissioning, certificates, homeowner guidance and a clear aftercare route

Scope and responsibilities are agreed for each project; not every development needs every technology.

OUR APPROACH

Designed in early. Delivered smoothly. Handed over simply.

Our aim is to make renewables an integrated part of the build, not a last-minute collection of subcontractor tasks. We agree interfaces early — including supplies, isolators, cable routes, plant locations and trade responsibilities — so each stage is easier to coordinate and the finished home is easier to understand.

GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

Can Holicon price an entire housing development?

Yes, we can discuss grouped plot packages, phasing and clearly defined installation responsibilities based on the project information supplied.

Do you work with self-build homeowners?

Yes. Early design conversations can help reserve the right roof and electrical provisions for solar PV, batteries and EV charging.

When should solar PV be considered on a new build?

Ideally during design and specification, before roof and electrical arrangements are finalised. Earlier coordination can reduce rework.

Can you help with documentation and handover?

Installation documentation, commissioning and relevant certification should be agreed as part of the project scope so every plot has a clear handover route.
