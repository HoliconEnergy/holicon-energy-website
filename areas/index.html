COMMON QUESTIONS

Do you install solar panels in smaller villages around Spalding?

Yes, smaller settlements around Spalding, Holbeach and Gedney are a core focus. Send us your postcode and a little about the project so we can confirm the next step.

Do you work with developers and commercial properties?

Yes. We can assess commercial buildings and housing developments, including coordinated renewable packages with defined scopes and handover requirements.

We also consider projects outside our core local area. Enquire with the address and requirements, and we'll confirm whether we can help.
