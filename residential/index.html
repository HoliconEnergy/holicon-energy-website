OUR APPROACH

What does a good home installation involve?

The right solution starts with a conversation about your property, consumption and longer-term plans. We can look at whether an initial solar installation, a battery-only project or a combined package makes the most sense.

What affects solar and battery value?

The benefit depends on your actual electricity usage, installation design, available roof space, import and export tariffs and how much electricity you use directly or store. Any savings estimate should be specific to your home rather than a guaranteed headline figure.

GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a battery with solar panels?

Not always. Solar panels can be installed without storage. A battery can help shift when you use electricity, but the right choice depends on your consumption, tariffs and budget.

Will solar panels work during a power cut?

Most standard grid-connected systems shut down during an outage. Backup operation requires compatible equipment and a correctly designed backup arrangement.

Can I add a battery to an existing system?

Often, yes. The options depend on your existing inverter, available space, electrical installation and what you need the battery to do.

How many solar panels should I install?

The useful starting point is your annual and daily electricity use, roof layout and future needs. We can assess a system rather than relying on a generic panel-count rule.
