Tesla support

Monitoring platform: Tesla app. These are safe first checks. Follow the instructions for your exact model when reconnecting Wi-Fi.

My monitoring app says offline

First check whether your broadband connection is working on another device. If your broadband provider, router, Wi-Fi name or password changed, your monitoring connection may need to be reconfigured. An offline app does not necessarily mean the solar system has stopped generating.

I changed my router or Wi-Fi password

Find the exact inverter or datalogger model and use its current manufacturer instructions. Connection methods vary by hardware revision. Many devices require a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network, but confirm this in your own model manual. Do not reset electrical equipment or change wiring to solve a network issue.

I can see an inverter warning or error code

Note the exact code, time, model and whether the system is otherwise operating. Do not remove covers or attempt internal electrical repairs. Contact Holicon or the manufacturer for model-specific guidance.

How do I understand my battery readings?

State of charge, reserve settings, import/export and charge schedules can affect readings. Check the app history before changing settings. If you suspect an unsafe battery condition, stop using the equipment as directed by the manufacturer and seek qualified assistance.

Wi-Fi reconnection: Support following a broadband/router/network change is outside Holicon's workmanship warranty and may be chargeable. We confirm charges before chargeable work. Genuine warranty concerns can still be reported.
