Commercial · Practical guide

Is Commercial Solar Worth It for UK Businesses?

How businesses can evaluate commercial solar using demand profiles, site constraints and operating costs.

Match generation to business demand

A site with strong daytime electricity use may consume a large share of solar generation directly. Review half-hourly data rather than relying on annual totals alone.

Check the building

Roof condition, structural suitability, access, asbestos considerations, fire strategy and electrical infrastructure should be reviewed.

Model the whole project

Include installation cost, finance, maintenance, export arrangements and realistic electricity-price assumptions.

Get a tailored assessment

Holicon can review commercial sites and discuss PV design, storage and installation sequencing.

Important: This is general guidance, not a site survey, funding decision or electrical design. Product compatibility and regulations should be checked for your installation.
