How Much Does a 10kW Solar System Generate in the UK?

What determines the annual and seasonal output of a 10 kWp solar array.

10 kW is a rated capacity

A 10 kWp array is the sum of the panels’ rated DC capacities under standard test conditions, not a promise of continuous 10 kW output.

Location and roof design matter

Orientation, tilt, shade and system losses affect output. Use a location-specific estimate instead of a single UK-wide figure.

Summer and winter differ

Longer summer days typically produce much more electricity than short winter days. Monthly estimates help set realistic expectations.

Use the electricity well

A demand profile, battery or EV charging schedule can help improve self-consumption, subject to equipment capability.

Commercial Solar Payback: A Practical Guide

The inputs that matter when estimating the financial return of commercial PV.

Payback is a model, not a guarantee

Simple payback divides net capital cost by estimated annual net benefit. The result changes if energy prices, usage or export terms change.

Self-consumption drives value

Electricity used on site typically avoids imported electricity charges; exported energy may be valued differently.

Include real costs

Account for roof works, grid connection, metering, maintenance, insurance and finance where applicable.

Test scenarios

Compare conservative, central and optimistic assumptions. We can help create a project-specific generation and consumption model.

G98 vs G99: What Does Your Solar Installation Need?

An introduction to UK grid connection routes for solar and battery installations.

Why grid rules matter

Generation connected to the public electricity network must comply with the applicable distribution network operator requirements.

G98 and G99 are different routes

The correct process depends on equipment type, phase, capacity and network conditions. Export limits and G100 schemes may also be relevant.

Do not assume export equals inverter size

Installed generation capacity, connection arrangement and approved export limitation are distinct considerations.

Plan applications early

Commercial and larger domestic projects may need DNO approval before installation. Holicon can review the likely route for your proposal.

How Many Solar Panels Do I Need?

A practical UK guide to sizing solar PV using electricity consumption, roof space and future demand.

Start with your electricity use

Look at annual electricity use in kWh rather than just your monthly bill. A home using 4,000 kWh a year does not automatically need a system producing exactly 4,000 kWh: when electricity is used matters too.

Estimate panel count

Modern panels commonly fall around 430–550 W each. Ten 480 W panels provide 4.8 kWp of rated capacity. Annual output depends on location, orientation, pitch and shading, so use a site-specific generation assessment.

Consider your roof and future plans

Allow for roof windows, setbacks, chimneys, shading and safe access. An EV or heat pump can increase future electricity demand. A battery may improve use of daytime generation, but does not increase what the panels generate.

What Holicon will check

We assess roof suitability, realistic generation, inverter selection, grid requirements and the best way to use your electricity. Ask for a tailored proposal rather than relying on a panel-count rule of thumb.

Can Solar Panels Work During a Power Cut?

Why standard solar systems disconnect during outages and what backup-ready designs involve.

Why standard systems shut down

Grid-connected inverters normally disconnect when the grid is unavailable to protect network workers and equipment.

What a backup system changes

A compatible inverter, battery and approved isolation/changeover arrangement may supply nominated circuits during an outage.

Understand the limitations

Available backup power is limited by inverter output, battery state of charge, starting currents and system configuration.

Plan before installation

Discuss critical loads, three-phase arrangements and the manufacturer-approved backup design with a qualified installer.

Solar Panels With or Without Battery Storage?

When a battery makes sense alongside solar PV and when panels alone may be suitable.

Panels generate; batteries shift

Solar panels produce electricity in daylight. A battery stores some surplus energy for later use, subject to system efficiency and capacity.

Your usage pattern matters

Homes empty during the day may use less solar directly than homes with daytime demand. Night-time tariffs and export arrangements can change the economics.

Size for realistic demand

An oversized battery can be expensive without providing much extra benefit. Review half-hourly consumption where possible.

Ask about backup separately

Not every battery supports power-cut backup. Check EPS and gateway requirements if resilience matters.

A clear starting point for understanding UK home energy funding without confusing grants and loans.

Start with the scheme, not the headline

Funding schemes change and eligibility can depend on location, household circumstances, property and the measures proposed. A loan must be repaid; a grant generally does not.

What Is a Battery Gateway — and Do I Need One?

Understand backup gateways, EPS and what happens to a home battery during a power cut.

A gateway is not the battery

A gateway or backup interface can help manage the electrical separation needed for a compatible battery system to supply selected circuits during a grid outage. Product names and functions vary by manufacturer.

Why batteries often stop during outages

Many grid-connected solar and battery systems are designed to shut down when the public grid fails. Having stored energy does not automatically mean you have backup power.

Whole-home versus essential-circuit backup

Some systems can support a dedicated essential-loads board; others can be designed for wider backup. Power, surge loads, phase arrangement and changeover design all matter.

Do you need one?

If uninterrupted power during an outage is important, discuss compatible backup equipment at design stage. If your goal is mainly tariff shifting and solar self-consumption, a backup gateway may not be necessary.
