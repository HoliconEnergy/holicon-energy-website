How Many Solar Panels Do I Need?

A practical UK guide to sizing solar PV using electricity consumption, roof space and future demand.

Start with your electricity use

Look at annual electricity use in kWh rather than just your monthly bill. A home using 4,000 kWh a year does not automatically need a system producing exactly 4,000 kWh: when electricity is used matters too.

Estimate panel count

Modern panels commonly fall around 430–550 W each. Ten 480 W panels provide 4.8 kWp of rated capacity. Annual output depends on location, orientation, pitch and shading, so use a site-specific generation assessment.

Consider your roof and future plans

Allow for roof windows, setbacks, chimneys, shading and safe access. An EV or heat pump can increase future electricity demand. A battery may improve use of daytime generation, but does not increase what the panels generate.

What Holicon will check

We assess roof suitability, realistic generation, inverter selection, grid requirements and the best way to use your electricity. Ask for a tailored proposal rather than relying on a panel-count rule of thumb.

Important: This is general guidance, not a site survey, funding decision or electrical design. Product compatibility and regulations should be checked for your installation.
